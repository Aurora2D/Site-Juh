About Juliana

With over 8 years of experience in the beauty industry, Juliana Callegaris brings the precision of Brazilian techniques to Florida. Specializing in nail architecture, lash lifting, and brow design, she creates tailored looks that enhance natural beauty.

“Beauty is in the details — I treat every client like a canvas, and my goal is to make you feel confident and radiant.”

Certified in multiple advanced techniques, Juliana stays ahead of trends to deliver flawless, long-lasting results.
